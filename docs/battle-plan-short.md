# Battle plan vs [AoA] — short version

**The situation.** They are stronger: 73B to our 67B, 17 squads over 1B to our 9, and four over 2B. Two of their squads (2.44B and 2.41B) can't be beaten by anyone we have, even after baiting, so we can't zero them and their Stronghold 1 is out of reach. Our ceiling is about 200★; a realistic full-day haul is 130–140★.

**How we win.** Take their Stars faster than they take ours. Every attack matters and every unused attack is a Star we didn't take. Attacks restore 1 per squad every 2 hours — use them as they come.

**Two rules for every attack.**
1. Only hit the stronghold we're on. A half-cleared stronghold unlocks nothing.
2. Only hit targets you beat outright: **your power ≥ 1.07× theirs** (× their current morale). A lost attack is only useful when it's a planned bait.
