# UFO attack bands vs [AoA]

Rule: you beat a target outright when **your power ≥ 1.07 × their power × their current morale**. At 100% morale that means targets up to about 93% of your own power. Every defender win drops their morale 10% (5% once below 80%, floor 60%), so a baited whale at 60% counts as 60% of its listed power — a 1.15B squad baited to the floor is a 690M target.

Front Wardens (5 Hearts): Stronghold 4 828M → needs 886M+ · Stronghold 5 770M → 824M+ · Stronghold 6 816M → 873M+.
