# Winter Siege: our plan and why

Full plan with every squad: https://raggylug233.github.io/winter-seige/

## How the event actually works

- Every squad in a stronghold is worth Stars: 1 at the front (4/5/6), 2 in the middle (2/3), 3 in the core (1). Wardens count double.
- The enemy takes those Stars only by beating a squad **four times** (five for a Warden). Beating it once or twice does nothing.
- They can't attack a stronghold until they've defeated **every** squad in the one in front of it. To reach Stronghold 1 they must fully clear a front stronghold *and* a middle one first.
- Every time a defender **wins**, its stats drop 10% (down to a floor of 60%). Enemies bait strong squads with weak attacks to wear them down, then finish with a whale. Losing a Heart resets it to 100%.
- Whoever has more Stars at the end wins (or whoever zeroes the other side first). No troops are lost.

## The plan in one line

**Whales guard the doors, the strong hold the middle, the weakest sit in the core.**

- **Fronts (4/5/6):** one whale Warden in each — Chytras, Raggylug, Leoder's 2nd squad — plus our mid-strength squads. A front can't be cleared without beating a 5-Heart whale five times, so an enemy that can't manage that never gets past the front at all.
- **Middle (2/3):** Leoder and Ms Get Down as Wardens with the next 18 strongest around them. If a front does fall, everything the enemy can reach next is worth 2 Stars and costs 15–30 attacks each.
- **Core (1):** our 20 lightest squads. It sounds backwards, but Stronghold 1 is the hardest place to reach, and a light squad there is 3 safe Stars instead of a cheap kill somewhere exposed.

## Why not the obvious "strongest in the core"?

Because the enemy chooses where to attack, and weak squads in a 3-Star stronghold are the best targets on the board. We tested this layout against the alternatives with a simulation of thousands of attacks: against an equal alliance it loses roughly a third fewer Stars than stacking the core, and against a weaker one about half.

## What this means for you

1. **Your placement isn't a judgement.** Being in the core doesn't mean you're weak, and being at the front doesn't mean you're expendable. Every slot is there for a reason.
2. **Where you sit doesn't change your attacks.** Everyone attacks at full strength from anywhere, and everyone attacks — even squads outside the top 20 or already defeated.
3. **Before Thursday 24:00 UTC:** gear on the heroes you registered. City bonuses, state positions and President skills don't count in this event, so don't spend on them for it.
4. **Don't leave the alliance during Preparation** — it drops your registration.
5. **On battle day, wait for the call.** We'll pick one enemy front, weak squads bait their strong defenders first, then the finishers go in. Use your attacks as they restore (every 2 hours); unused attacks are the most common way to lose.
