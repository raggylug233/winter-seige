# Winter Siege deployment strategy

Written 2026-09-16 for the 6-stronghold, 20-slot battlefield with 108 squads (54 players). Rules and sources are in `research.md`; the assignment is generated by `build.py` into `index.html`; `sim.py` compares layouts.

## Rules the plan rests on (from the in-game Rules Overview)

- **Unlocking:** every squad in a stronghold must be defeated before the strongholds it connects to can be attacked. Map paths: 4 → 2, 5 → 2 and 3, 6 → 3, then 2 and 3 → 1. So the enemy breaks *one* front, then the Advanced stronghold behind it, then Stronghold 1.
- **Stars:** Primitive 1 per squad, Advanced 2, Empowered 3; Wardens double. A squad's Stars are lost only when all its Hearts are gone (normal 4, Warden 5).
- **Morale:** defender stats = lock-time stats × morale. Each defender *win* costs 10% above 80%, then 5%, floor 60%. Losing a Heart resets it to 100%. Attackers are always at 100%.
- **Attacks:** 10 per squad over the day (3 at start, +1 every 2 h, max 7 restored). ~1,080 per side for 54 players, against ~486 Hearts on a full board.
- **Result:** first to zero the enemy wins; otherwise most Stars left; then surviving troop power.

## What the second research pass added

Twenty-one creator videos (July 12 – Sept 17, 2026) were read in full. Nobody has seen our layout: every guide describes 4 strongholds × 30 squads, and "Advanced" strongholds appear nowhere, so the plan comes from the rules plus simulation. Findings that survived:

- Attackers behave the same everywhere: average each front's power, break the cheapest one, then farm the highest-Star squads they can reach cheaply. Bottom-tier attackers bait morale on strong defenders, mid players take the weakened target, whales finish. Eliminated and non-garrisoned squads still attack.
- The Warden is exempt from the top-20 power cut and is chosen from the stronghold's top 5. Moving a Warden's squad drops the appointment; re-appoint after moving.
- Post-change data (WOS Strategy Lab, Sept 13): an alliance with 33% stronger squads started 12 Stars behind because it deployed 22 squads vs 36. Every empty slot is a forfeited Star.
- Old advice to discard: "only register strong players" (the tiebreak used to be lock-time garrison power; now it's *surviving* power, so weak squads cost nothing and add Stars).
- The creators' morale floor (35%) is wrong for our game: the in-game text says 60%. That makes strong squads much harder to remove: a defender 1.7× stronger than the enemy's best attacker can't be killed at all.

## How the attacker sees it

A weak squad dies in 4–5 attacks wherever it is. A squad stronger than most enemy attackers costs baits first: with the 60% floor, 15–30 attacks for a 1B+ squad against an equal enemy. Stars per attack is what a rational attacker maximises, so:

- weak squads in Stronghold 1 or 2/3 are the best targets on the board (0.5–0.75★ per attack);
- weak squads at the fronts are the worst (0.2★ per attack), and two of the three fronts are usually never attacked at all, so their Stars are safe;
- strong squads are a *toll*, and the toll only counts on the path the enemy actually takes.

Because the enemy chooses the path, strength at the fronts is one-third efficient (two fronts sit idle), strength in the Advanced ring is one-half efficient, and strength in Stronghold 1 is fully efficient but only paid after 17 + 42 = 59★ are already gone.

## Simulation

`sim.py` runs an enemy (our own roster, scaled by a power factor) that respects the unlock paths, wins an attack with probability rising with its power advantage, and baits morale when it can't win outright. Two attacker behaviours are modelled: **coordinated** (commit to the cheapest stronghold to clear, then the next one on the path) and **greedy** (always hit the best Stars-per-attack target reachable, which spreads attacks across fronts and then farms whatever opens). Every layout below is balanced by snake draft, 16 runs each. Stars lost (of 198) by attacks each enemy squad actually lands (they have 10; sleep and wasted attacks push the real number down):

|---|---|---|---|

| graded30 (1–30 in 2/3, 31–78 at fronts) | 56–66 / 87–91 / 110–154 | 70–124 / 111–156 / 171–181 | 80–127 / 134–164 / 183–190 |

Each cell is coordinated–greedy. Reading it:

- **Against an equal or weaker enemy, graded wins clearly**: 46–65★ lost at 3 attacks per squad and 88–94 at 4, versus 84–97 and 118–127 for "strongest in the core". This is the range where the battle is actually decided.
- **Against a 15–30% stronger enemy, the layout stops mattering much.** Their top squads beat ours outright (the whole of Stronghold 2, whales included, fell in ~130 attacks in one trace), so nothing we garrison is a toll any more and only the Heart count on the path slows them. All layouts lose 130–190★ once they land 4+ attacks per squad. The greedy attacker punishes the weak Stronghold 1 (63★ for ~90 attacks once an Advanced stronghold is down), which is why graded's greedy column is worse than "strongest in the core" by 10–20★ at low budgets; the coordinated column still favours graded. That 10–20★ is the price of the 20–40★ gain against equal opponents, and against a clearly stronger enemy no placement changes the result.
- When the enemy lands 8+ attacks per squad, every layout is wiped: 486 Hearts is simply below their budget, and the event is a race to zero decided by attack speed, not placement.

A "fortress" layout (strongest 48 at the fronts) was also tested and is the worst under path gating (60 / 138 / 174 against an equal enemy): the enemy breaks one front and finds only cheap 2★ and 3★ squads behind it.

## The plan

| Stronghold | Tier | Squads | Who | Warden | Stars |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Advanced | 20 | ranks 1–20 (10, snake with 3) + ranks 69–88 (10) | strongest present | 19×2 + 4 = 42 |
| 3 | Advanced | 20 | same | strongest present | 42 |
| 4 | Primitive | 16 | ranks 21–68, snake 4→5→6→6→5→4 | strongest present | 15 + 2 = 17 |
| 5 | Primitive | 16 | same | strongest present | 17 |
| 6 | Primitive | 16 | same | strongest present | 17 |
| 1 | Empowered | 20 | ranks 89–108 | strongest present | 19×3 + 6 = 63 |

Total 198 Stars. The 12 unused slots are at the fronts (16 of 20 each), where a squad is worth 1★; Strongholds 1–3 are full.

Why each piece:
- **Whales in 2 and 3.** Once a front falls, the only reachable squads are worth 2★ and cost 15–30 attacks each. The enemy's best option becomes breaking a second front for another 17★, which is fine.
- **Mid squads at the fronts.** A front falls no matter what, so its Stars (17) are the price of admission; what we control is the toll. Ranks 21–68 (470–680M) cost the enemy's mid squads real attacks and morale, without wasting whales two-thirds of the time. All three fronts are balanced so there's no cheap door.
- **Weakest 20 in Stronghold 1.** They'd be the enemy's favourite targets anywhere else. Here they are 60★ that only come into play after a front *and* an Advanced stronghold are fully cleared. The known weakness: against an enemy strong enough to walk through the whales, those 63★ go quickly. But an enemy that strong takes the rest too, whatever we do.
- **Ranks 69–88 in 2/3.** They fill the Advanced ring to 20 (2★ each) and are protected by the whales next to them: the enemy still has to spend a front's worth of attacks to reach them.

Things that will feel wrong but aren't:
- Leoder, Ms Get Down and Chytras are Wardens worth 4★ in 2/3 rather than 6★ in Stronghold 1. Those extra Stars only exist if the enemy reaches Stronghold 1; the wall is what stops that.
- The Empowered Warden is a ~300M squad worth 6★. If the enemy is at Stronghold 1 the battle is already lost.
- Placement changes nothing about attacking; every squad attacks at full strength from wherever it is garrisoned.

Assumptions still to verify in-game before lock:
1. Advanced Warden = 4 Stars (double, like the other tiers).
2. Warden must be in the stronghold's top 5 by power. Satisfied automatically, since every Warden here is the top squad of its stronghold.

## Operating notes for R4/R5

- Use **Manage Squads → batch adjust** during Preparation (Wed–Thu UTC). Everything locks at Thursday 24:00 UTC.
- Re-appoint Wardens after moving squads; moving a Warden's squad drops the role.
- Anyone who leaves the alliance during Preparation loses their registration and must re-register. Cut off alliance-hopping well before lock.
- Members must have their gear on the registered heroes at registration; stats are frozen at lock. City bonuses, state positions, President skills, territory buffs and exclusive-gear *skills* don't count. Pet skills are already on.
- Battle day: the race is tight, so attack throughput matters. Don't attack at reset; scout their fronts, average each one's power, pick the weakest, agree the bait → kill order, and get every squad's attacks used as they restore (every 2 h).
