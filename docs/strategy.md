# Winter Siege deployment strategy

Written 2026-09-16, revised 2026-09-17, for the 6-stronghold, 20-slot battlefield with 112 squads (56 players). Rules and sources are in `research.md`; the assignment is generated by `build.py` into `index.html`; `sim.py` compares layouts; `comparison-gemini.md` compares this plan with the one Gemini produced.

## Rules the plan rests on (from the in-game Rules Overview)

- **Unlocking:** every squad in a stronghold must be defeated before the strongholds it connects to can be attacked. Map paths: 4 → 2, 5 → 2 and 3, 6 → 3, then 2 and 3 → 1. So the enemy breaks *one* front, then the Advanced stronghold behind it, then Stronghold 1.
- **Stars:** Primitive 1 per squad, Advanced 2, Empowered 3; Wardens double. A squad's Stars are lost only when all its Hearts are gone (normal 4, Warden 5).
- **Morale:** defender stats = lock-time stats × morale. Each defender *win* costs 10% above 80%, then 5%, floor 60%. Losing a Heart resets it to 100%. Attackers are always at 100%.
- **Attacks:** 10 per squad over the day (3 at start, +1 every 2 h, max 7 restored). ~1,120 per side for 56 players, against ~500 Hearts on a full board.
- **Result:** first to zero the enemy wins; otherwise most Stars left; then surviving troop power; then whoever dropped to that number later. Equal Stars before and after means both lose.

## What the research added

Twenty-one creator videos (July 12 – Sept 17, 2026) were read in full. Nobody has seen our layout: every guide describes 4 strongholds × 30 squads and "Advanced" strongholds appear nowhere, so the plan comes from the rules plus simulation. Findings that survived:

- Attackers behave the same everywhere: average each front's power, break the cheapest one, then farm the highest-Star squads they can reach cheaply. Bottom-tier attackers bait morale on strong defenders, mid players take the weakened target, whales finish. Eliminated and non-garrisoned squads still attack.
- The Warden is exempt from the top-20 power cut and is chosen from the stronghold's top 5. Moving a Warden's squad drops the appointment; re-appoint after moving.
- Post-change data (WOS Strategy Lab, Sept 13): an alliance with 33% stronger squads started 12 Stars behind because it deployed 22 squads vs 36. Every empty slot is a forfeited Star.
- Old advice to discard: "only register strong players" (the tiebreak used to be lock-time garrison power; now it's *surviving* power, so weak squads cost nothing and add Stars).
- The creators' morale floor (35%) is wrong for our game: the in-game text says 60%, which makes strong squads much harder to remove. A defender 1.7× stronger than the enemy's best attacker can't be killed at all.

## How the attacker sees it

A weak squad dies in 4–5 attacks wherever it is. A squad stronger than most enemy attackers costs baits first: with the 60% floor, 15–30 attacks for a 1B+ squad against an equal enemy. Stars per attack is what a rational attacker maximises, so:

- weak squads in Stronghold 1 or 2/3 are the best targets on the board (0.5–0.75★ per attack);
- weak squads at the fronts are the worst (0.2★ per attack), and two of the three fronts are usually never attacked at all, so their Stars are safe;
- strong squads are a *toll*, and the toll only counts on the path the enemy actually takes;
- a front Warden is special: it is the one squad that *must* die before the Advanced ring opens. A 5-Heart whale there is the most expensive gate on the map.

## Simulation

`sim.py` runs an enemy (our own roster, scaled by a power factor) that respects the unlock paths, wins an attack with probability rising with its power advantage, and baits morale when it can't win outright. Two attacker behaviours are modelled: **coordinated** (commit to the cheapest stronghold to clear, then the next one on the path) and **greedy** (always hit the best Stars-per-attack target reachable, which spreads attacks across fronts and then farms whatever opens). All layouts are balanced by snake draft, 16 runs each. Stars lost (of 202) by attacks each enemy squad actually lands (they have 10; sleep and wasted attacks push the real number down), shown as coordinated / greedy at 3 · 4 · 5 attacks:

Reading it:

- **Front Wardens are the single most valuable placement.** Against a weaker uncoordinated enemy they cut losses roughly in half (39–48★ vs 58–88★ for the same plan without them): the enemy farms cheap 1★ squads at the fronts and never opens the Advanced ring. Against an equal enemy they cost nothing.
- **A whale Warden in Stronghold 1 doesn't help.** The "balanced" plan puts Leoder there; in the traces the enemy still clears a front and an Advanced stronghold, then farms the 19 weak squads around him for 63★. The Stronghold 1 Warden is 6★ that only exist if the enemy has already beaten 90 squads.
- **Against an enemy 15%+ stronger, layout stops mattering.** Their top squads beat ours outright, nothing we garrison is a toll, and every layout loses 130–190★ once they land 4+ attacks per squad. Placement decides close matchups.
- When the enemy lands 8+ attacks per squad, every layout is wiped: ~500 Hearts is below their budget, and the event is a race to zero decided by attack speed.

A "fortress" layout (strongest 48 at the fronts) was also tested and is the worst under path gating (60 / 138 / 174 against an equal enemy): the enemy breaks one front and finds only cheap 2★ and 3★ squads behind it.

## The plan

| Stronghold | Tier | Squads | Who | Warden | Stars |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Advanced | 20 | rank 1 + ranks 6–23 (9, snake with 3) + ranks 73–92 (10) | Leoder S1 | 19×2 + 4 = 42 |
| 3 | Advanced | 20 | rank 2 + same | Ms Get Down S1 | 42 |
| 4 | Primitive | 18 | rank 3 + ranks 24–72 (snake 4→5→6→6→5→4) | Chytras S1 | 17 + 2 = 19 |
| 5 | Primitive | 17 | rank 4 + same | Raggylug S1 | 18 |
| 6 | Primitive | 17 | rank 5 + same | Leoder S2 | 18 |
| 1 | Empowered | 20 | ranks 93–112 | strongest present (~300M) | 19×3 + 6 = 63 |

Total 202 Stars. The 8 unused slots are at the fronts, where a squad is worth 1★; Strongholds 1–3 are full.

Why each piece:
- **Whale Wardens at the fronts.** The only way into the Advanced ring is through a front, and the only way to clear a front is to kill its Warden five times. Chytras, Raggylug and Leoder's second squad are the toll booths; anyone who can't bait them down to 60% and beat them there never gets past.
- **Leoder and Ms Get Down anchor 2 and 3**, with the next 18 strongest around them. Once a front does fall, the reachable targets are worth 2★ and cost 15–30 attacks each.
- **Mid squads at the fronts.** A front's Stars (18–19) are the price of admission if the enemy can pay it; ranks 24–72 (~450–690M) make the rest of the front cost real attacks without wasting whales two-thirds of the time. All three fronts are balanced so there's no cheap door.
- **Weakest 20 in Stronghold 1.** They'd be the enemy's favourite targets anywhere else. Here they are 60★ behind two gates. The known weakness: an enemy strong enough to walk through the wall takes those 63★ quickly — but that enemy takes everything anyway.
- **Ranks 73–92 in 2/3.** They fill the Advanced ring to 20 (2★ each) and sit behind a front whale and next to the wall.

Things that will feel wrong but aren't:
- Chytras, Raggylug and Leoder's S2 are worth 2★ as front Wardens rather than 3★/6★ in Stronghold 1. Those Stars only exist if the enemy reaches Stronghold 1; a front whale is what stops that.
- The Empowered Warden is a ~300M squad worth 6★. If the enemy is at Stronghold 1 the battle is already lost.
- Placement changes nothing about attacking; every squad attacks at full strength from wherever it is garrisoned.

Assumptions still to verify in-game before lock:
1. Advanced Warden = 4 Stars (double, like the other tiers).
2. Warden must be in the stronghold's top 5 by power. Satisfied automatically, since every Warden here is the top squad of its stronghold.
3. Mai ♡ and CANSæRBæRæ (four squads) come from Gemini's list, not from our screenshots; their power (790M / 626M and 624M / 495M) should be checked in-game.

## Operating notes for R4/R5

- Use **Manage Squads → batch adjust** during Preparation (Wed–Thu UTC). Everything locks at Thursday 24:00 UTC.
- Re-appoint Wardens after moving squads; moving a Warden's squad drops the role.
- Anyone who leaves the alliance during Preparation loses their registration and must re-register. Cut off alliance-hopping well before lock.
- Members must have their gear on the registered heroes at registration; stats are frozen at lock. City bonuses, state positions, President skills, territory buffs and exclusive-gear *skills* don't count. Pet skills are already on.
- Battle day: the race is tight, so attack throughput matters. Don't attack at reset; scout their fronts, average each one's power, pick the weakest, agree the bait → kill order, and get every squad's attacks used as they restore (every 2 h).
