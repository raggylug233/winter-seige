SIN vs [HoT]

Too close to call at equal activity — whoever uses more of their attacks wins.

Commentary (16 October)

Too close to call, and SIN is the underdog where it counts. SIN has more total power (69.0B vs 60.4B), but HoT has the whales: 10 squads over 1B vs SIN's 5, the strongest squad (1.58B vs 1.34B), and a 1.21–1.58B Warden in every stronghold. At 6 attacks landed per squad the simulation has HoT losing 102–137★ and SIN about 135★: whichever side uses more of its attacks wins on Stars.

SIN's bottleneck: one finisher

Only one SIN squad, the blank-named player's #1 (1.34B), can kill HoT's Wardens. Each Warden has 6 Hearts and that squad has 7–10 attempts, so it can take one Warden, not two.

Recommended plan for SIN

  1. Clear Stronghold 5 first. Falcon #2 is the weakest gate, and clearing 5 opens both 2 and 3. Split the finishing hits between blank #1 and GuGu #1, with weak squads taking the deliberate losses before each finishing hit.
  2. Farm Strongholds 2 and 3: 37 squads worth 2★ that plenty of SIN squads beat outright, about 185 attacks for 74★. Best value on the board.
  3. Then the non-Warden squads at Strongholds 4 and 6, at 1★ each.
  4. Skip HoT's Stronghold 1. Reaching it means killing BRWDKS or Nneo, and SIN won't have the finisher attempts left.

Realistically SIN can take about 130★, so it is a race: use every attempt as it restores and never attack a squad you don't beat outright.

Defence

HoT's top squads beat every SIN squad outright (Falcon #1 alone beats anything up to about 1.48B), so no SIN stronghold is safe. A SIN win depends on out-attacking HoT, not out-defending them.

Phase 6 — if Stronghold 3 is fully cleared (~167 attacks): farm Stronghold 1

17 squads worth 3★ that we beat outright or nearly: ~85 attacks for 51★. Their Stronghold 1 can be zeroed.
