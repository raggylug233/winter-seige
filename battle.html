Their weakest Warden (jinx S1, 770M, 5 Hearts) and it unlocks BOTH 2 and 3. About 81 attacks for 21★. Mid squads (500–800M) take the rank 2–10 squads; anything under 400M is a job for our 450–600M squads. The Warden needs attackers over 824M — no baiting needed, we have plenty.

Phase 2 — farm the cheap 2★ squads in Strongholds 2 and 3

31 squads worth 2★ each that 12+ of our squads beat outright: ~124 attacks for 62★. This is the best Stars-per-attack on their board. Do NOT waste attacks on their whales here yet.

Phase 3 — clear Strongholds 4 and 6

About 81 + 81 attacks for 42★. Same squad tiers as Phase 1. Wardens Macsen S2 (828M) and Mistyy S1 (816M) need attackers over 886M.

Phase 4 — mid 2★ targets in 2 and 3

2 squads that 3–11 of our squads beat outright, or that need a bait or two first: ~8 attacks for 4★.

Phase 5 — only if attacks are left: the hard 2★ targets

6 squads that need 4–6 baits per Heart before one of our top squads can finish: ~123 attacks for 12★. Poor value; do it with leftover attempts. NoSoup4u S2 (their SH3 Warden) can only be finished by Leoder S1 at 60% morale and is what stands between us and their Stronghold 1 — not worth it.
