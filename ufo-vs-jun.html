UFO vs [JUN]

UFO is favoured: at equal activity we keep more Stars than they do.

Commentary (16 October)

UFO is clearly favoured. JUN is much weaker (48.8B vs 72.3B, 3 squads over 1B vs our 13) and has fewer squads (108 vs 130), so fewer attacks too. The first side to zero the other wins outright, and we have the attacks to do it.

UFO attackingJUN attacking
Attempts at 7 per squad130 × 7 = 910108 × 7 = 756
Needed to clear the other side completely~570~1,100

Even at 7 attempts per squad, we can zero JUN with about 340 to spare, and JUN can't zero us even if every attempt lands.

Attack order

  1. Clear Stronghold 4 (~91 attacks). It opens their Stronghold 2. Warden xsharman #2 (850M) is beaten outright by 18 of our squads.
  2. Farm Stronghold 2's 2★ squads: 17 squads for 34★. Their Warden there, xsharman #1 (1.08B), is for the 8 finishers who beat it outright.
  3. Clear Stronghold 6, then farm Stronghold 3. Boss_ #2 (1.04B) is beaten outright by 8 of ours; Franka #1 (813M) by 20.
  4. Finish on Stronghold 1 (57★) once 2 or 3 is clear. Warden Frank1119 #1 (974M) is beatable.
  5. Leave Stronghold 5 for last. Boss_ #1 (1.41B) is beaten outright by none of ours. Only Leoder #1 can kill it, after one deliberate loss per Heart: about 30 attacks for 2★, and Stronghold 5 opens nothing that 4 and 6 don't.

Rules to remember

  • Hearts are now 5 per squad and 6 per Warden; morale drops only 5% per defensive win above 90%, then 2%. Baiting a squad down is mostly wasted attempts: only attack squads you beat outright (see "beat outright by" below).
  • Caveat: our side uses the planned garrison, not screenshots of what was locked in. If our in-game layout differs, this page needs re-running.
